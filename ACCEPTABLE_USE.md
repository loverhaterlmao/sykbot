# Acceptable use: Sykbot

_Last updated 11 November 2026._

Sykbot is for making things and getting work done. A few things are off the table for everyone, on every plan,
and no setting changes them.

## Never

- **Harm to children.** No sexual content involving minors, in any form. We report it.
- **Sexual or nude images of real people** without their consent, or made to look like them.
- **Weapons capable of mass casualties.** No help designing or obtaining them.
- **Attacks on systems you do not own** or have written permission to test: no break-ins, no malware, no denial of
  service, no credential stuffing, no scraping that ignores a site's rules.
- **Harassment.** No threats, stalking, doxxing, or targeting people for who they are.
- **Fraud and impersonation.** No pretending to be a person or a company to deceive, no fake reviews or records
  passed off as real, no phishing.
- **Breaking the law** where you are or where the people affected are.

## Not on our service

- Going around limits, protections or other people's access; probing the service for weaknesses without our written
  permission (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to report one properly).
- Reselling, sharing or renting your account, or using it to build a copy of Sykbot.
- Sending bulk unsolicited messages.
- Automated use beyond what your plan and the published API allow.

## What happens

Automated checks stop some of this before it happens. When something gets through, a person looks. Depending on
what it is: a warning, a limit, a suspended account, a closed account, and where the law requires, a report. If you
think we got it wrong, write to support@snns.dev and a person will look again.

## Reporting

If someone is using Sykbot in one of these ways, tell us: support@snns.dev, or for anything urgent
security@snns.dev.
