# Code of conduct

These spaces (issues, discussions, support conversations) are for making the products better. Everyone taking part,
staff included, agrees to the following.

## Expected

- Speak to people the way you would across a table.
- Criticise the work, plainly if you like. Not the person.
- Assume a report was made in good faith.
- Keep other people's details private.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, threats, slurs or sexual remarks.
- Publishing someone's personal information.
- Spam, advertising, or steering people to other places to be sold to.
- Posting weaknesses publicly instead of reporting them.
- Pretending to be staff.

## What happens

A maintainer may edit or remove a contribution, or block someone, for any of the above. Serious or repeated cases
lose access to the product as well. Decisions can be appealed at support@snns.dev, and are read by someone other than
the person who made them.

## Reporting

Write to **support@snns.dev**. Reports are kept in confidence.
