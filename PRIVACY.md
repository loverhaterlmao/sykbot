# Privacy: Sykbot

_Last updated 11 November 2026._

This is the short version, for people reading the repository. The policy published at https://sykbot.com is the one that
binds; if the two ever differ, the site's is right and this page is out of date. Tell us: privacy@snns.dev.

## What we keep, and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your account: an email address, a name you choose, how you sign in | So you can sign in and we can reach you about your account |
| What you put into Sykbot | To give it back to you; it is yours |
| A record of sign-ins and security events, with the network address they came from | To keep accounts safe and to stop abuse |
| What you write to support | To answer you |
| If you pay: what you bought and when | Our accounts, and your plan |

Each is kept only as long as that purpose needs it. The policy on the site gives the periods.

Card details are typed into the payment company's own form. We never see or keep them.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not ask for more than a feature needs.

## Who else handles it

Companies that run parts of the service for us (hosting, the network in front of our sites, mail, payments), under
agreements that bind them to use it only for that. Law enforcement, only when the law requires it of us.

## Protecting our sites

Our sites watch for automated attacks. An address that probes for weaknesses can be blocked across all S&NS sites
for a time. That record holds the address and what it did, not who you are.

## Your choices

Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it: privacy@snns.dev, from the address on
your account. We answer within the time the law gives us. If you are in the EU, the UK, California or anywhere else with similar law,
you have the rights that law gives you and we honour them the same way for everyone.

## Children

Sykbot is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep information about them.

## Changes

When this changes in a way that matters, we say so on the site before it takes effect.
