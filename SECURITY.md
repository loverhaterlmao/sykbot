# Security

Found a weakness in Sykbot? Thank you for telling us before anyone else.

## How to report

Write to **support@snns.dev** with the subject "Security report: Sykbot", or use the form at
[noetic.lol/support](https://noetic.lol/support) and choose *A security report*. Reports are read by the owner and
admins only. Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Say what you found, where, how to see it again, and what it would let someone do.

## The ground rules

- Test only against your own account and your own data, and stop at proof.
- No floods, load tests or automated scanning. Addresses that scan are shut out automatically and reported.
- No social engineering of staff or other users.
- Give us reasonable time to fix a problem before you talk about it.

Keep to these and we will not pursue you for good-faith research, and we will credit you if you want it. There is no
paid bounty at the moment.

The full policy, scope and what protects your account: [SECURITY.md in S&NS Suite](https://github.com/loverhaterlmao/snns-suite/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
