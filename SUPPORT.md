# Support

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Ask how something works | sykbot.com has its own help; or write to **support@snns.dev** |
| Report something broken | Open an issue here, or use the form at [noetic.lol/support](https://noetic.lol/support) |
| Report a security weakness | See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Not in a public issue |
| Send a legal or privacy notice | **support@snns.dev** with "Legal" in the subject |

A person reads every message. Most are answered within two working days.

**Support will never ask for** your password, a sign-in or recovery code, card details, or payment to anyone's
personal account. If someone claiming to be S&NS does, do not answer, and tell us.
